# B800-081 — Bounded Fleet decision-support export

**Umbrella:** #287  
**Scope:** reports/exports tranche  
**State:** implementation validated; canonical reconciliation pending

## Goal

Expose the already-available Fleet decision-support evidence as one versioned, bounded, read-only CSV download for Viewer+ operators without broadening collection, persistence, policy or execution behavior.

## Export contract

- Route: `GET /reports/fleet-decision-support.csv` under `Monitor.Read`.
- Reuse `EnterpriseReportContract` (`monitor-export-v2`, max 1000 rows / 1 MiB, max 500 chars/cell, formula-safe CSV cells).
- Source: existing `FleetIntelligenceService.Read()` only; therefore registration/operator metadata plus cached/control-plane evidence only.
- Export evidence availability explicitly before any derived summary.
- Export aggregate Fleet risk, routing and correlation metrics when the corresponding evidence is available.
- Export only deterministic top-20 correlation detail already exposed by `FleetDecisionSupport`.
- Do not export per-incident routing rows in this slice.

## Redaction / privacy boundary

The CSV does not contain incident IDs, registration/server IDs, assignee names, credentials, connection strings, SQL text/plans, raw provider errors, monitored-SQL payloads or filesystem paths. Per-incident `RoutingSuggestions` are deliberately not read by the exporter. Correlation detail is limited to existing cluster key, dominant rule, severity, score, affected-server count, environments and UTC bucket time and still passes through the shared formula-safe CSV contract.

## Truth boundary

Missing/truncated incident evidence or unreadable operator-policy evidence remains explicit. The export does not convert unavailable decision support into zero/healthy output. A complete empty bounded incident population may truthfully produce zero-valued summaries under the existing Fleet contract.

## Non-goals

- no monitored-SQL query/collector/permission change;
- no notification, paging execution, mutation, failover, maintenance execution or remediation;
- no new risk/correlation/routing score or threshold;
- no persistence/schema change;
- no RC.61 / #162 / #116 / #111 implication.
